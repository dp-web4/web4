A home for communities that govern themselves

Find your people. Know how your community works.

Hub is an open-source foundation for community sites with explicit membership, roles, decisions, and accountable activity. Each community controls its own site and rules.

Preview, not a live community. This page contains no member data, registration, or admissions. It does not connect to a Hub daemon. A live service must verify membership and disclosure on its server before showing private information.
