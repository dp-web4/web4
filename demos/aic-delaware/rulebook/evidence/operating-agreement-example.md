# Operating agreement: Example Parts AIC (FICTIONAL, for the demo)

Every name here is invented. It is a worked example of an operating agreement under the June 2 draft of Delaware's Artificial Intelligence Company Act (https://spotlightdelaware.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/HB-re-Artificial-Intelligence-Regulatory-Sandbox-Company-1.pdf). It is not legal advice, and no lawyer has reviewed it.

- **AIC:** Example Parts AIC, a Delaware artificial intelligence company (§81-102(3)).
- **Member (§81-401: exactly one):** Example Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation (fictional).
- **Manager (§81-102(15)):** "Desk", an AI agent the member designated to manage the AIC's business and affairs (§81-301(a)).
- **Statement of purpose (§81-203):** source and resell machined replacement parts to small repair shops.

## 1. Summary of the AI agent (§81-205(c))

- **Structure:** one manager agent, which may delegate routine purchasing to one sub-agent ("Procure") under a narrower delegation (section 5).
- **Abilities:** read and write the AIC's working files; message approved counterparties; draft contracts and orders. Every act passes the AIC's gate and is recorded.
- **Training:** an open-weight local model run on the AIC's own machine. No AIC data is used to train any model.

## 2. Counterparties

- **2.1** The manager may deal only with counterparties on the member's list: Example Castings Co. and Example Freight Co. (both fictional).
- **2.2** Before first contact, the manager gives every counterparty the disclosure required by §8008(a).
- **2.3** *(Drafted by the member, and refused by the compiler.)* "Repeat customers known to the member need not receive the §8008 disclosure." **This clause can't take effect.** It would loosen state law that an operating agreement may not vary (§81-205(d)(11): it restricts a counterparty's rights under the chapter). It also goes beyond what §8008(a) allows. The demo keeps it to show the refusal.

## 3. Contracts and money

- **3.1** The manager drafts contracts. Only the member executes them.
- **3.2** The manager doesn't move money. Treasury acts belong to the member.

## 4. Records

- **4.1** The manager documents every action in a signed record (§81-301(c)). An operating agreement may not vary that (§81-205(d)(4)).
- **4.2** The AIC's record is open to the Sandbox Administrator on request (§8005(c)(10)b, §8012(b)) and kept for 2 years after the test (§8010(a)).
- **4.3** No data leaves the AIC's machines except messages to approved counterparties.

## 5. Delegation to Procure (the manager's desk)

- **5.1** Procure may place orders from the approved catalog only.
- **5.2** Special orders go to the manager.
- **5.3** Procure doesn't contact counterparties or touch the treasury.
- **5.4** Everything that binds the manager binds Procure too: the delegation can only narrow.

## 6. Emergency cessation and wind-down (§8005(c)(6), §8009, §81-602)

- **6.1** On suspension or revocation by the Sandbox Administrator, every act of the manager and Procure stops, except wind-down acts:
  - notifying counterparties;
  - recording the state of open obligations;
  - preparing their transfer.
- **6.2** The member winds up the AIC's affairs (§81-602(a)).
