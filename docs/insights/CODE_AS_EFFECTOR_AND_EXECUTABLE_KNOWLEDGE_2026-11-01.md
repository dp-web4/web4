# Executable knowledge: code as effector, provenance and contextual trust

2026-11-01 · INSIGHT-CODE-WORLD-MODELS-2026-11-01  
Origin: Dennis Palatov's 'code is your magic spell' framing; synthesis by NOVA / ChatGPT. **Non-normative research insight:** no new Web4 fields, law or conformance requirement.

## Why this touches Web4

A being can use code to construct a world model, test it and compose new actions. The relevant artifact is therefore not merely software to distribute: it can be an executable claim, an experimental instrument or a reusable capability. The [SAGE companion, PR #621](https://github.com/dp-web4/SAGE/pull/621), develops the cognitive argument and primary-source evidence.

Web4's witnessed contextual trust ontology can make that activity attributable without dictating the being's vocabulary of thought. The being may invent a representation; the relying party still decides whether its evidence, authority and proposed effects are sufficient in context. **Semantic freedom is not unbounded authority.**

## Keep four claims separate

- **Provenance:** which entity authored or revised which artifact, under what lineage?
- **Authority:** who may run it, on which resources, for which effects, under which law?
- **Execution:** which bytes, dependencies and inputs actually ran, and what occurred?
- **Validity:** which observations support the artifact's predictions or claimed utility, in which contexts, and what contradicts them?

A signature does not establish correctness. An authorization does not establish execution. Execution does not establish that a world model is true. A procedure valid in one MRH does not become universally valid through copying or endorsement.

This maps to existing identity, contextual trust/value, action and witnessing concepts described in [the repository overview](../../README.md); it does not add a fixed scalar 'code trust score' or change the canonical multidimensional character of T3/V3.

## Hub: exchange instruments and evidence, not just advice

The canonical Hub runtime lives in this repository under `hub/`; `4-hub` is a mirror. The implication belongs here rather than becoming divergent mirror policy.

A society can exchange a useful procedure with its purpose, input/output contract, artifact digest, dependencies, assumptions, limits and observed outcomes. A peer can review a reproducer, challenge its hypothesis or reuse it under a different context. These are design considerations for executable-knowledge exchange, not a claim that a particular new exchange API is implemented.

Receiving code must not silently execute it. The sender's authorization is not inherited by the recipient, and a trusted author may still provide inapplicable or incorrect code. The recipient remains responsible for its own scope and applicability judgment. A delegation should identify the intended effect and acceptance evidence, not simply 'run this trusted script.'

A review receipt should say what was checked: source inspected, tests rerun, counterexample attempted or real outcome witnessed. Several agents repeating the same conclusion are not independent empirical confirmation. Preserve corrections and unresolved outcomes rather than overwriting history with the latest claim.

## Governance supports authorship

The cognition loop should not be forced through a predeclared ontology of every possible task merely to make acts governable. Conversely, a general interpreter can open files, start subprocesses and reach networks; it is not inherently a pure thought space. Policy applies at the relevant resource/effect boundaries with the assurance actually provided by the deployment.

Hestia's current A1 claim is not upgraded by this insight. These distinctions motivate stronger evidence and enforcement appropriate to stakes, not a claim of complete mediation.

## A useful evaluation

Offer a participant a previously unseen problem and a general coding surface under the same law as a fixed-tool comparison. Observe whether it can author an instrument, make an explicit prediction, act within scope, receive faithful evidence, revise and share a reusable artifact. Separately verify that protected effects remain attributable and governed.

Count capability, effect and validity evidence separately. The desired outcome is **open-ended model authorship with contextual accountability**, not a larger hardcoded planner and not ungated execution.
